What I Believe

These are the truths that ground everything I do.

Your body was never the problem

You were taught to override your instincts and shrink yourself down. But your body has always been your wisest teacher. It holds everything you need to come home to yourself.

Healing is not linear

Some days you'll feel like you've cracked it open. Other days you'll curl up. Both are part of the process. There is no timeline and no finish line. Just the next breath.

You belong here

Queer, creative, neurodivergent, messy, loud, quiet. Whoever you are and however you show up, there is space for you. You don't need to perform wellness to deserve healing.

Community heals

We were never meant to do this alone. Being witnessed from a non-judgmental space changes everything. When we breathe together, something shifts that can't happen in isolation.

Liberation lives in the exhale

Breathwork is a decolonial practice. It's about reclaiming your body and your voice, and taking back your agency. You release the conditioning and the weight that was never yours to carry.

Trust the body's wisdom

Your nervous system holds the answers. When you slow down enough to listen, your body will tell you exactly what it needs.

What I won't do

  • I won't shame you into showing up. You'll come when you're ready
  • I won't pretend there are only 3 spots left to make you panic-book
  • I won't poke at your wounds to sell you the cure
  • I won't hide behind jargon or spiritual bypassing
  • I won't push past your boundaries

Hold me to this. It's a living practice.

You don't need to be fixed.
You need to be felt.
