More about Me

I am Pip. She/they — a white queer bodied woman. I am a British Breathwork, Movement and Voice Facilitator, a singer, dancer and a lover of music, nature, adventure and all the colours of expression that we live in that makes up a soulsome life.

I was born in the north east of England, in a market town that sits in-between the city of York and the coastline of the North Sea, close to the vast moors, villages and valleys.

As I remember, I have always been a curious deep soul with a fun side — and my journey of late has been a return and a remembrance.

Breathwork entered my life during the beginning of a major life transition, initially ignited the moment I seriously asked spirit to take the lead. And here I found myself in a messy, eye-opening awakening — the crossroads showed up in many parts of my life.

The layers of inner work have been very revealing, griefy and insightful. And as my nervous system embodies more safety and trust, with clearer body communication, the people pleaser part of me has had a very much needed slow death.

The knock-on effect of this work is the agency and freedom to breathe, move and voice as my full self. And I want this for you too!

My Approach

How I Work

I support people to reconnect with parts of themselves that got buried by people-pleasing and conforming to social norms.

My approach is decolonial and embodied. Warm, but fierce. I create containers that honour the fullness of your experience, without trying to fix you or rush you through it.

This work helps you trust your body and its intuition. It builds safety inside you, and a fuller relationship with your soul self and with life.

I hold space for people who have spent too long making themselves smaller. Maybe you're a musician carrying the weight of performance. Maybe a creative, burned out from producing. Or you're queer and unlearning what the world taught you about your body. There is room for you here.
